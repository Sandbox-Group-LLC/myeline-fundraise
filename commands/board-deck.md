# Board Deck

Boards are working sessions. The deck is an agenda wearing a format.

## Memory Protocol

1. **Open with commitments.** Pull last board meeting's facts: every
   commitment made, its status. First section of the deck: promises kept,
   changed, or missed — with why. This continuity is what buys a board's
   trust in everything after it.
2. **Metrics from the record.** The standard panel (growth, retention, burn,
   runway, pipeline) pulled from logged facts with sources. Bad numbers
   appear with the response plan, never hidden.
3. **Three decisions.** A board works when it decides. Name the three
   decisions this meeting must make, each with options and your
   recommendation.
4. **Risks, owned.** The top risks with mitigation owners and dates.
5. **LAND IT BACK.** Commitments made at the meeting land as facts with
   owners and dates — next meeting opens from them.

Keep it to what a director can hold: one page per section, the narrative in
the room, not on the slide.
