# Fundraise Plan

Fundraising is a sales process: the product is equity, the customer is a VC
partner, the close is a term sheet. Plan it like one.

## Memory Protocol

1. **Readiness from the record.** Assess against the 13 proof points
   (traction, PMF signals, unit economics, team, narrative, why-now) using
   the brain's facts — not the founder's optimism. Output the readiness
   scorecard with evidence links; gaps become the pre-raise workstream.
2. **Target list from history.** Pull every logged investor interaction:
   who's warm, who's said no (and why — a no with a reason is a next-round
   lead), who's in diligence. The target list starts from remembered
   relationships, not a cold database.
3. **Sequence for momentum.** Parallel-first-then-compress: build the funnel
   wide, create competitive dynamics, converge meetings into a tight window
   so conviction compounds. Anchor dates to real milestones logged in the
   brain.
4. **LAND IT BACK.** The plan itself becomes facts: targets and stage,
   timeline anchors, the readiness gaps to close. Every investor meeting
   during the raise logs its outcome as a fact — the funnel report then
   writes itself, and a stalled raise is visible in the data, not in vibes.

Include the pre-raise checklist (data room, references, the narrative
locked, the Q&A bank drilled) and the abort criteria — knowing when to stop
a raise and get back to building is strategy too.
