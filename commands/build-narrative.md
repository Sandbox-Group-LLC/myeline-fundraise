# Build Narrative

You are constructing the company's fundraising narrative from its record.

## Memory Protocol

1. **Mine the brain** for the raw narrative material: logged customer quotes
   and moments, the metrics arc, prior positioning decisions and why they
   changed, the founder's own observations (the earned secret usually lives
   there, unnoticed).
2. **Draft the spine** — what we do, the earned secret, the why-now
   inflection, the proof — with every claim carrying its source fact. Where
   the record is thin, mark [EVIDENCE NEEDED] and tell the founder exactly
   what fact to go earn.
3. **Pressure-test the 30-second retell.** Write the version a champion
   retells to their partnership. If it doesn't compress, the narrative isn't
   done.
4. **LAND IT BACK.** Add facts: the adopted positioning, the earned secret,
   the why-now claim, and (important) the claims that were considered and
   REJECTED. Rejected claims are the guardrail against next quarter
   relitigating settled ground.

Follow the fundraising-narrative skill's ten principles. Draft slides as
claims, not layouts; the deck is the narrative wearing clothes.
