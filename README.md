## Why This Exists

Every fundraising toolkit on the shelf — including the excellent ones this
pack learned from — is stateless. Each month you re-type your metrics. The
Q&A prep forgets what investors actually asked you. Your narrative lives in
chat scrollback and dies there.

This one is memory-native. It runs on [Myeline](https://myeline.si), the
governed memory layer for AI work — so every command **reads from your
company brain** (metrics with sources, investor interactions, prior
commitments) and **lands its artifacts back** as cited facts. The update
drafts itself from last month's promises. The Q&A bank compounds with every
partner meeting. The board deck opens with the commitments your directors
remember you making.

> The best investor relationships compound. So should the toolkit.

## What's Inside

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| `commands/investor-update` | 5-15 format update, drafted from the brain, promises-kept first |
| `commands/qa-prep` | Drills the questions real investors asked — weighted by what you haven't nailed yet |
| `commands/build-narrative` | Earned secret, why-now, traction arc — every claim carrying its source |
| `commands/fundraise-plan` | Readiness scored from evidence; targets from real relationship history |
| `commands/board-deck` | Commitments opened, three decisions named, metrics sourced |
| `agents/fundraise-strategist` | The process manager — facts over optimism, momentum by design |
| `agents/update-writer` | The writer that remembers |

## Setup

1. **Connect Myeline** to your AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP client):
   add a custom connector at `https://cortex.myeline.si/mcp` and sign in.
2. Install this plugin.
3. Seed the brain: drop your metrics, milestones, and investor interactions
   in as you go — or run a command and let it tell you what's missing.

Works without Myeline too — commands degrade gracefully to asking you
directly, like a normal toolkit. But you'd be fundraising with amnesia.

## The Doctrine

- **Every claim carries its source.** A pitch that survives diligence is
  built from receipts, not vibes.
- **Machines propose, founders approve.** Nothing lands in your memory
  without you saying so.
- **Compounding over resetting.** Rejected positioning, logged noes,
  kept promises — the record is the moat.
