# Case Study: The Toolkit That Remembers

*A pitch-deck-ready story, proven live on November 17, 2026 — every claim
with its receipt.*

---

## The Setup (the problem everyone lives with)

Every AI toolkit on the shelf is stateless. The founder re-types their
metrics every month. Q&A prep forgets what investors actually asked. The
pitch narrative lives in chat scrollback and dies there. The tools got
smart; the memory didn't.

## The Earned Secret

**Agents that remember change the SHAPE of the work, not just its speed.**
A stateless tool answers questions. A memory-native tool makes the work
compound: this month's update opens from last month's promises, the Q&A
bank hardens with every partner meeting, the board deck starts from
commitments the directors remember making.

You can't bolt this onto a chatbot. It requires governed memory — facts
with sources, approved by humans, refusing to guess. Which is exactly what
Myeline is.

## The Live Drill (receipts on file)

Ran in production on a real Myeline tenant, November 17:

promised last month — every number cited to a fact a human approved. The
